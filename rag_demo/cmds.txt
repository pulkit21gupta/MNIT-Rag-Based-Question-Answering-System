----------------------------------------------------------------
 HOW THE HYBRID SEARCH WORKS
----------------------------------------------------------------

    Every question triggers TWO searches in parallel:

    1. ChromaDB (local) -- searches your 12 MNIT .txt files
    2. Tavily (web)     -- searches mnit.ac.in only (official)

    Both results are combined and passed to the Groq LLM,
    which generates one unified answer.

    In the chat UI:
      Teal chips  = local knowledge base sources
      Green chips = official mnit.ac.in web sources (clickable)
