---SECTION: OFFICE OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: What is the Office of International and Alumni Affairs at MNIT Jaipur?
The Dean International Affairs and Alumni Affairs Office handles all international partnerships and alumni activities of MNIT Jaipur. Its purpose is to promote international and intercultural exposure among faculty and students, and to build a lifelong, mutually beneficial relationship with alumni.

---SECTION: INTERNATIONAL AFFAIRS---

TOPIC: What does the International Affairs office do at MNIT Jaipur?
The International Affairs office:
- Promotes partnerships with foreign universities/institutions through MoUs.
- Focuses on Student Exchange, Faculty Exchange, and Joint Research.
- Facilitates academic mobility programs and collaborative teaching.
- Manages admissions for international students (via DASA and MEA nominations).
- Provides opportunities for students to gain global exposure and knowledge of international technologies.

TOPIC: With which countries does MNIT Jaipur have academic MoUs?
MNIT Jaipur has established MoUs with universities across: Canada, USA, UK, Germany, Japan, South Korea, Hungary, Russia, Australia, Nigeria, Turkey, Finland, and Mauritius.

TOPIC: Which universities are partner institutions of MNIT Jaipur?
Key partner universities:
- University of Saskatchewan (Canada)
- University of Calgary (Canada)
- Loughborough University (UK)
- Coventry University (UK)
- North Carolina University (USA)
- North Dakota State University (USA)
- Jeonbuk National University (South Korea)
- OTH Amberg-Weiden (Germany)
- Hiroshima University (Japan)
- Eötvös Loránd University (Hungary)
These MoUs enable student and faculty exchange, joint research projects, and collaborative teaching initiatives.

TOPIC: How are international students admitted to MNIT Jaipur?
International students can be admitted through:
- DASA (Direct Admission of Students Abroad) – for NRIs, PIOs, and foreign nationals via JEE Main score.
- Ministry of External Affairs / ICCR nominations – supernumerary seats for students nominated by MEA.
- Study in India (SII) program.
Eligibility: Class 12 equivalent with minimum 60% aggregate (or SAT scores for DASA).

TOPIC: From which countries do international students come to MNIT Jaipur?
International students at MNIT Jaipur come from Saudi Arabia, UAE, Yemen, Bangladesh, Nepal, Uganda, Sri Lanka, and other Middle Eastern and South Asian countries.

---SECTION: ALUMNI AFFAIRS---

TOPIC: What is MNITJAA?
MNITJAA stands for MNIT Jaipur Alumni Association. It maintains alumni-institute interaction through a global alumni network and alumni chapters worldwide. It organizes annual alumni meets and felicitation ceremonies. Recent events include the Industry Institute Interaction (I³) meeting (April 2025) and the Diamond Jubilee batch reunion (1965–1970 batch, March 2025).

TOPIC: How can MNIT alumni stay connected with the institute?
Alumni can register on the official alumni portal: https://alumni.mnit.ac.in/
They can join the MNIT Jaipur Alumni Association (MNITJAA) and participate in alumni meets, mentorship programs, and ALCOM (Alumni Committee) activities. Alumni contribute across fields such as Academics, Science & Engineering, Public Service, Corporate, Defence, and Arts & Design.

TOPIC: Can alumni book the MNIT Guest House?
Yes. Registered and verified alumni can book the MNIT Guest House via https://guesthouse.mnit.ac.in/ using their alumni portal login credentials. Profile verification takes 2–3 working days after registration at alumni.mnit.ac.in.

---SECTION: STUDENT EXCHANGE---

TOPIC: Can MNIT students go for exchange programs abroad?
Yes. Through MoUs with partner universities, MNIT Jaipur students can participate in student exchange programs, research internships, and academic mobility programs. Students should contact the Office of International Affairs at mnit.ac.in/intaffairs/ for current opportunities.

TOPIC: Does MNIT Jaipur have international students on campus?
Yes. International students are enrolled at MNIT Jaipur from multiple countries. They are admitted through DASA, MEA/ICCR nominations, and the Study in India program.
