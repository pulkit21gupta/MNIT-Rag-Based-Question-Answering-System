# Academic Section

## Overview

The Academic Section at MNIT Jaipur is headed by the Dean Academic and is responsible for implementing academic decisions approved by the Senate, SUGB, and SPGB.

### Main Responsibilities

* Maintain UG, PG, and PhD academic records
* Manage curriculum and academic calendar
* Handle registration and course enrollment
* Conduct supplementary examinations
* Issue certificates and transcripts
* Manage scholarships and assistantships
* Handle academic communication between departments and students
* Maintain records for Senate, Convocation, SUGB, and SPGB meetings
* Process fee-related matters and refunds
* Handle RTI and academic correspondence

# FAQ Dataset

# General FAQs

## Q1. Is MNIT Jaipur affiliated with any university?

MNIT Jaipur is an autonomous Institute of National Importance under the Ministry of Education, Government of India.

## Q2. Can MNIT Jaipur award degrees?

Yes. MNIT Jaipur can award undergraduate and postgraduate degrees under the NIT Act 2007.

## Q3. Is branch sliding allowed after first year?

No. Internal branch sliding or branch change is not allowed after first year admission through CSAB.

## Q4. Is lateral entry available for diploma students?

No. Lateral entry is not available for diploma or B.Sc. students.

## Q5. Is management quota available?

No. MNIT Jaipur does not offer management quota admissions.

## Q6. Is NRI quota available?

No. However, foreign students can apply through DASA.

---

# Admission FAQs

## Q1. How can students take admission in B.Tech or B.Arch?

Admission is through CSAB counseling after qualifying JEE Main.

## Q2. How can students take admission in M.Tech or M.Plan?

Admission is through CCMT counseling or institute-sponsored admission process.

## Q3. How can students take admission in PhD?

Admission is through institute selection conducted twice every year.

## Q4. How can students take admission in M.Sc?

Admission is through CCMN counseling using IIT JAM score.

## Q5. How can students take admission in MBA?

Admission is through institutional selection based on advertisement released every year.

---

# Certificate FAQs

## Q1. How can students obtain transcripts or duplicate certificates?

Students must apply using the prescribed format and pay the required fee.

## Q2. Is there any fee for first-time issue of grade sheets or migration certificates?

No. First-time issue is free.

## Q3. How can students get bonafide certificates?

Students can apply using the prescribed application format available on the academic website.

## Q4. What is the CGPA to percentage formula?

Reference formula:
Percentage = CGPA × 10

---

# Scholarship FAQs

## Q1. Are scholarships available for SC/ST/OBC/EBC students?

Yes. Eligible students can apply for Post Matric Scholarships offered by government agencies.

## Q2. Can a student avail multiple scholarships simultaneously?

No. Only one government scholarship can be availed at a time.

## Q3. Do M.Tech students with GATE score receive assistantship?

Yes. Full-time M.Tech students admitted through CCMT receive institute assistantship subject to attendance and CGPA requirements.

## Q4. Are part-time students eligible for scholarship?

No. Part-time students are not eligible for institute assistantship.

## Q5. What is the minimum CGPA requirement for PhD assistantship?

PhD assistantship is terminated if CGPA becomes less than 7.

---

# Registration FAQs

## Q1. Is semester registration compulsory?

Yes. Every student must register every semester.

## Q2. Is physical presence required during registration?

Yes. Students must sign the registration register physically.

## Q3. What happens if a student misses registration?

The student cannot attend classes or examinations.

## Q4. Is late registration allowed?

Yes, with late fee, only within permitted dates.

## Q5. Does fee payment alone complete registration?

No. Physical verification and signature are mandatory.

---

# Academic FAQs

## Q1. How can students add or drop courses?

Students can add or drop courses through ERP within dates specified in the academic calendar.

## Q2. Can students take open electives from their own department?

No. Open electives from the same department are not allowed.

## Q3. How many supplementary subjects are allowed?

Maximum three subjects.

## Q4. Are F grades counted in CGPA?

Yes. F grades are included in CGPA and SGPA calculations.

## Q5. Can students audit courses?

Yes. Audited courses are not included in CGPA.

---

# Important Rules

* Internal branch sliding is not allowed.
* Supplementary examination is allowed only once for a subject.
* Maximum 3 subjects can be registered in supplementary exams.
* Students absent continuously for 3 weeks may have semester registration cancelled.
* Physical registration is compulsory.
* Part-time students are not eligible for assistantship.
* F grades are included in CGPA calculations.
* Open electives from own department are not allowed.
