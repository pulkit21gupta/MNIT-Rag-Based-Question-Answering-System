---SECTION: OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: What is the administrative structure of MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur is governed by a Senate (academic policy body) and a Board of Governors (BoG – administrative body). The Director is the head of the institute and serves as ex-officio Chairman of the Senate. Under the Director, several Deans head key functional areas.

---SECTION: GOVERNANCE BODIES---

TOPIC: What is the role of the Senate at MNIT Jaipur?
The Senate is the academic governing body. It controls and approves curriculum, courses, examinations, and results. It appoints committees for academic matters and reviews teaching, training, and research. The Director serves as ex-officio Chairman. Members include all Professors, Deans, and external educationists/specialists.

TOPIC: What is the Board of Governors at MNIT Jaipur?
The Board of Governors (BoG) is the top administrative authority of MNIT Jaipur. It consists of members nominated by the NIT Council, the Director, and representatives from MoE (Ministry of Education), Government of India. The Visitor (President of India) is the highest authority in the NIT organizational structure.

TOPIC: Who funds MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur is fully funded by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Government of India. Annual funding is approximately US$20 million from the Government of India and the World Bank combined. Alumni and institutional bodies also contribute.

TOPIC: What is the motto of MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur's motto is "Yogah Karmasu Kaushalam" (Sanskrit: योगः कर्मसु कौशलम्), meaning "Excellence in action is Yoga" – sourced from the Bhagavad Gita, Chapter 2, Verse 50.
