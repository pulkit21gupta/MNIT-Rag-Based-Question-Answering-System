---SECTION: OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: How many guest houses are there at MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur has two guest houses on campus:
1. INDRADHANUSH (GH-1) – 15 Deluxe Rooms (Ground Floor: 5, First Floor: 10) + 1 Meeting Room
2. SURYODAYA (GH-2) – 28 Standard Rooms (Ground Floor: 8, First Floor: 10, Second Floor: 10)
Both are fully air-conditioned. The Indradhanush Guest House also includes a meeting room.

TOPIC: What amenities are provided in MNIT Guest House rooms?
Deluxe Rooms (Indradhanush GH-1):
- 24-hour running hot and cold water
- Free Wi-Fi
- TV with cable
- Telephone service
- Laundry service
- Big-sized rooms
- Tea-making facility (in-room)

Standard Rooms (Suryodaya GH-2):
- 24-hour running hot and cold water
- Free Wi-Fi
- TV with cable
- Telephone service
- Laundry service

TOPIC: Does the MNIT Guest House serve food?
Yes. The guest house offers vegetarian food only — breakfast, lunch, and dinner. Takeaway is also available for MNIT staff. Orders must be placed in advance.
- Breakfast: INR 100 (order at least 1 day before)
- Lunch/Dinner: INR 200 each (order at least 4 hours before)

---SECTION: ROOM CHARGES (VERIFIED FROM OFFICIAL PORTAL)---

TOPIC: What are the charges for Deluxe Rooms at MNIT Guest House?
Deluxe Room rate: INR 950/night (rack rate)
- Category A (BOG/FC/Senate members, NIT Directors, Ministry Committees): Free
- Category B (Institute Guests on invitation, Workshop/Conference participants): INR 475/night
- Category C (MNIT Alumni self or spouse, Staff's personal guests): INR 665/night
- Category D (Faculty from other CFTIs): INR 760/night
- Category E (Parents of enrolled students, on Head/Warden recommendation): INR 950/night

TOPIC: What are the charges for Standard Rooms at MNIT Guest House?
Standard Room rate: INR 700/night (rack rate)
- Category A (BOG/FC/Senate members, NIT Directors, Ministry Committees): Free
- Category B (Institute Guests on invitation, Workshop/Conference participants): INR 350/night
- Category C (MNIT Alumni self or spouse, Staff's personal guests): INR 490/night
- Category D (Faculty from other CFTIs): INR 560/night
- Category E (Parents of enrolled students, on Head/Warden recommendation): INR 700/night

---SECTION: BOOKING & ELIGIBILITY---

TOPIC: Can parents of MNIT students book the guest house?
Yes, but only parents of currently enrolled MNIT Jaipur students can book under Category E. No other relatives, siblings, or friends are allowed. The student's ID must be uploaded at booking, and the student must be physically present at check-in to verify the guests' identity.

TOPIC: How do I book the MNIT Guest House?
Online booking portal: https://guesthouse.mnit.ac.in/
Alumni must first register at https://alumni.mnit.ac.in/ and get their profile verified (takes 2–3 working days) before booking through the guest house portal.

TOPIC: What is the maximum booking period at MNIT Guest House?
Maximum booking is 7 consecutive days. For extension beyond 7 days, approval from the Registrar or Guest House Coordinator is required.

TOPIC: What is the cancellation policy for MNIT Guest House?
- Cancellation 30–7 days before arrival: 5% of total rent charged as cancellation fee.
- Cancellation 7–2 days before arrival: 10% of total rent charged as cancellation fee.
- Key must be collected on the first day; otherwise booking is auto-cancelled for subsequent days.

---SECTION: RULES & CONTACT---

TOPIC: What are the rules at MNIT Guest House?
- Consuming alcohol is strictly prohibited in the guest house.
- Only one room booking allowed per alumni.
- Booking may be cancelled if the institute requires the room for urgent official purposes.
- Only one room per alumni booking. 

TOPIC: What is the contact for MNIT Guest House queries?
Guest House queries: guesthouse@mnit.ac.in
Alumni portal queries: alumniaffairs@mnit.ac.in
Booking portal: https://guesthouse.mnit.ac.in/
