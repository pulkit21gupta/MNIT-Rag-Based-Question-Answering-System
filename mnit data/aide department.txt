---SECTION: DEPARTMENT OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: What is the AIDE department at MNIT Jaipur?
The Department of Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering (AIDE) at MNIT Jaipur is a dedicated department focused on the fields of Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and Data Engineering. It combines AI principles with data engineering practices to prepare graduates for real-world challenges in technology and industry.

TOPIC: When was the AIDE department established at MNIT Jaipur?
The AIDE department is a relatively new department at MNIT Jaipur, introduced as part of the institute's expansion into emerging technology fields under the New Education Policy (NEP 2020).

TOPIC: What is the vision of the AIDE department?
To create a center of international standards for imparting high-quality education and conducting research in Artificial Intelligence, Data Engineering, and related fields to meet the current and future challenges of technological development.

TOPIC: What is the mission of the AIDE department?
To create an enabling environment for excellent teaching, learning, and research in order to produce leading entrepreneurs and innovators in the field for sustainable development and the betterment of the nation.

---SECTION: ACADEMIC PROGRAM---

TOPIC: What course does the AIDE department offer?
The AIDE department currently offers a B.Tech. (Bachelor of Technology) in Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering at the undergraduate level. The department plans to soon offer Postgraduate (M.Tech.), Doctoral (Ph.D.), and other cutting-edge programs combining AI principles with data engineering practices.

TOPIC: What is the duration of the B.Tech AIDE program at MNIT Jaipur?
The B.Tech. in Artificial Intelligence and Data Engineering is a 4-year full-time undergraduate degree program.

TOPIC: How many seats are available in the AIDE B.Tech program?
The B.Tech. AIDE program has a seat intake of 40 students per year.

TOPIC: What subjects are covered in the AIDE curriculum?
Key subjects and topics covered in the AIDE curriculum include:
- Artificial Intelligence and Expert Systems
- Machine Learning (Linear models, Logistic Regression, Neural Networks, Gradient Descent, Bias-Variance Tradeoff, Regularization, Cross-Validation)

- Data Mining (Classification, Clustering, Sequence Mining, Web Mining, Link Analysis)
- Digital Image Processing
- Information Retrieval and Search Engines
- Recommender Systems
- Python Programming
- Computational Learning Theory
- Multimedia Information Retrieval

TOPIC: What are the future programs planned for the AIDE department?
The department plans to offer Postgraduate (M.Tech.), Doctoral (Ph.D.), and other advanced programs that combine AI principles with data engineering. These are expected to be launched soon.

---SECTION: ADMISSION & ELIGIBILITY---

TOPIC: How do I get admission to AIDE B.Tech at MNIT Jaipur?
Admission to B.Tech. AIDE is through JEE Main, followed by JoSAA (Joint Seat Allocation Authority) counselling. Steps:
1. Appear in JEE Main and obtain a valid All India Rank (AIR).
2. Register on the JoSAA portal and fill preferences.
3. Participate in multiple seat allotment rounds.
4. Accept the allotted seat, pay the fee, and report to MNIT Jaipur.

TOPIC: What is the eligibility criteria for B.Tech AIDE at MNIT Jaipur?
- Must have passed Class 12 (10+2) with Physics, Chemistry, and Mathematics (PCM).
- General/EWS Category: Minimum 75% aggregate marks in Class 12 or top 20 percentile of their board.
- SC/ST/PwD Category: Minimum 65% aggregate marks in Class 12.
- A valid JEE Main score is mandatory.

---SECTION: FEES---

TOPIC: What is the fee for B.Tech AIDE at MNIT Jaipur?
Total tuition fee for the complete 4-year B.Tech. AIDE program: approximately INR 2,50,000 (as per NIRF 2024 data). The annual tuition fee for 2024–25 is approximately INR 5 lakhs (total across all semesters).

TOPIC: Are there any fee waivers for AIDE students at MNIT Jaipur?
Yes:
- SC/ST/PwD students and students with family income below INR 1 lakh per annum: Full tuition fee waiver.
- Students with family income between INR 1 lakh and INR 5 lakh per annum: Two-thirds tuition fee waiver.

---SECTION: INSTITUTE OVERVIEW (MNIT JAIPUR)---

TOPIC: What is MNIT Jaipur?
Malaviya National Institute of Technology (MNIT) Jaipur is a premier public technical institute established in 1963 as Malaviya Regional Engineering College. It was upgraded to NIT status on 26 June 2002 and declared an "Institute of National Importance" under the NIT Act on 15 August 2007. It is located in Jaipur, Rajasthan, on a 317-acre campus.

TOPIC: What is the NIRF ranking of MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur is ranked 42nd–43rd in the Engineering category by NIRF 2024–2025. It is ranked 18th by India Today (2024) among 284 BTech colleges.

TOPIC: Is MNIT Jaipur accredited?
Yes. MNIT Jaipur is accredited by NBA (National Board of Accreditation) and approved by MHRD and AICTE.

TOPIC: What is the campus size of MNIT Jaipur?
MNIT Jaipur is spread across approximately 317 acres in Jaipur, Rajasthan, at JLN Marg, Malviya Nagar.
