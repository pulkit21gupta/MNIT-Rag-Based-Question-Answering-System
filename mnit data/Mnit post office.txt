---SECTION: OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: Is there a post office inside MNIT Jaipur campus?
Yes. MNIT Jaipur has a post office located within the campus premises. It is part of the western campus zone along with the departmental buildings, bank, canteen, and administrative block.

TOPIC: What services does the MNIT campus post office offer?
The on-campus post office provides standard India Post services:
- Sending and receiving letters and parcels
- Speed post and registered mail
- Money orders
- Postal deliveries for students and staff on campus

TOPIC: Who can use the MNIT campus post office?
The post office is open for use by all students, faculty, and staff of MNIT Jaipur.

TOPIC: Where is the post office on the MNIT campus?
The post office is on the western side of the MNIT campus, near the departmental buildings, administrative block, ICICI Bank, and canteen areas.

TOPIC: How useful is the on-campus post office for new students?
The on-campus post office is very useful for new students for sending and receiving important documents such as admit cards, scholarship letters, certificates, and official correspondence, without needing to travel outside campus.
