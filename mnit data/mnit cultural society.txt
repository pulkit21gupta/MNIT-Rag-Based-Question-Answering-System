---SECTION: OVERVIEW---

TOPIC: What is CACS at MNIT Jaipur?
CACS stands for Creative Arts & Cultural Society. It is the official cultural body of MNIT Jaipur. It provides students a platform to explore talent, develop leadership skills, and build teamwork. CACS operates with student teams under the guidance of faculty advisors and hosts 18 active clubs that meet regularly throughout the academic year.

TOPIC: What does CACS do at MNIT Jaipur?
CACS organizes a wide array of cultural events and activities throughout the year across both semesters. The best performances and entries are showcased at the annual cultural fest, Blitzschlag. CACS helps students grow professionally and personally through planning, public speaking, and teamwork.

---SECTION: CLUBS UNDER CACS---

TOPIC: What are the active clubs under CACS at MNIT Jaipur?
CACS hosts 18 active clubs. Confirmed clubs include:
- Classical Music & Dance Club – Indian classical traditions (tabla, harmonium, classical dance forms)
- Dramatics Club – Drama in Hindi and English; acting, directing, set design, writing
- English Literary Club – MUN, creative writing, book reviews, storytelling
- Film & Photography Club
- Fine Arts Club
- Western Music Club
- Dance Club (Western/fusion styles)
- Think India Club – group discussions, debates, social and national issues, Sewa activities
- Travel & Heritage Club – hiking, cycling tours, heritage site documentation around Jaipur
- Electoral Literacy Club – civic awareness and electoral rights education
- Vighyan Club (Science)
- Srijan Club (Creative/Technical)
- And other active clubs throughout the year.

---SECTION: MAJOR EVENTS---

TOPIC: What is Blitzschlag at MNIT Jaipur?
Blitzschlag is the annual cultural fest of MNIT Jaipur. It is a 3-day event held in early February every year. The word "Blitzschlag" has German origins meaning "lightning strike." The fest attracts over 20,000–30,000 visitors from more than 20 colleges across India. It features cultural performances, competitions, seminars, workshops, exhibitions, and quizzes. It is organized by students under faculty guidance. Blitzschlag'26 was held from February 6 to 8, 2026.

TOPIC: What is Sphinx at MNIT Jaipur?
Sphinx is the annual technical fest of MNIT Jaipur. It is a 3-day event held in late October every year. It features technical competitions, hackathons, workshops, and guest lectures organized autonomously by students under faculty guidance.

TOPIC: What other events does MNIT celebrate?
- MST (MNIT Sports Tournament) – annual invitational sports event
- AIAMBT (All India Advani Memorial Basketball Tournament) – professional basketball invitational
- Mahamana Pandit Madan Mohan Malaviya Jayanti – celebrated on December 25 every year
- Various inter-branch and club-level events throughout both semesters.
