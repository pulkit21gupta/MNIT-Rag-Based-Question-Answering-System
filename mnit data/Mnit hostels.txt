SECTION 1: OVERVIEW AND TOTAL CAPACITY
=======================================================================

MNIT Jaipur provides on-campus boarding and lodging facility to all regular students including UG, PG, and PhD scholars. As per the official MNIT hostel page, the total accommodation capacity is 3058 for boys and 1509 for girls. The messes in all hostels are managed and regulated by the MNIT Mess Council.

There are a total of 15 hostels on the MNIT Jaipur campus. Out of these, 11 hostels are for boys and 4 hostels are for girls. Boys hostels are located on the eastern side of the campus. Girls hostels are located separately with dedicated wardens and facilities.

The newest hostel for boys is Aravalli Hostel, which has the best room quality and infrastructure among all boys hostels. Ganga Hostel is among the newest additions for girls.

SECTION 4: HOSTEL FEE STRUCTURE (2025-26, FROM OFFICIAL REGISTRATION GUIDE)
=======================================================================

The following fee structure is from the official MNIT UG Registration Guide 2025-26 PDF published on mnit.ac.in. All amounts are in Indian Rupees (INR).

Room Rent per semester: INR 6,000
Light and Water Charges per semester: INR 6,000
Total Room Rent plus Light and Water Charges per semester: INR 12,000

Mess Advance per semester: INR 25,000. The mess advance is adjusted against actual consumption at the time of leaving the hostel.

Hostel Caution Money: INR 12,000. This is charged only once at the time of first allotment and is fully refundable at the time of leaving the hostel permanently.

One-Time Admission Fee for Hostel and Mess: INR 3,000. This is non-refundable.

Total first-semester hostel fee (new students): INR 52,000 approximately, which includes room rent, light and water charges, mess advance, caution money, and one-time admission fee.

For continuing students (each semester): approximately INR 37,000 including room rent plus light and water (INR 12,000) and mess advance (INR 25,000).

Hostel fees must be paid online only. Cash payments are not accepted. Payment can be made through Internet Banking, Debit Card, or Credit Card. Demand Draft in favour of MNIT Mess Council payable at Jaipur is also accepted.

SECTION 5: HOSTEL ALLOTMENT PROCESS (STEP-BY-STEP, VERIFIED FROM OFFICIAL 2025-26 GUIDE)
=======================================================================

The hostel allotment process at MNIT Jaipur follows four official steps as described in the UG Registration Guide 2025-26.

STEP 1 — WILLINGNESS:
During the filling of the Admission Form on MNIT ERP, the student must give hostel willingness by selecting the option Hosteller under the Student Details tab. The option to choose is either Day Scholar or Hosteller. The hostel will be allotted randomly in terms of branch and state of domicile to all willing students who are not from Jaipur city. Students from Jaipur city are not eligible for hostel allotment and will be asked to surrender their rooms and apply for refund if allotted.

STEP 2 — FEE PAYMENT:
After hostel allotment, the student must deposit hostel fee online only through the MNIT ERP portal. The fee portal opens and closes on specific dates announced by the hostel office each year. For example, for session 2025-26 the portal was open from 08 August 2025 to 25 August 2025. Payment can be made through Internet Banking, Debit or Credit Card, or Demand Draft in favour of MNIT Mess Council payable at Jaipur. Cash is strictly not accepted.

STEP 3 — ROOM NUMBER INTIMATION:
After receipt of the hostel fees, the hostel office will send the student an email through ERP with the allotted Hostel Room Number within 24 hours.

STEP 4 — POSSESSION OF HOSTEL ROOM:
Upon arrival on campus, the student can check into the allotted room on a 24x7 basis by showing the allotment email on mobile phone along with a government-issued photo ID to the hostel caretaker.

FOR EXISTING HOSTELLERS (CONTINUING STUDENTS):
Existing hostellers must give Hostel Willingness each semester through the ERP before the deadline announced by the hostel office. A separate circular is issued each semester for Hostel Willingness for the upcoming semester. If the willingness is not submitted, the student may not be allotted a hostel room for that semester.

FOR PG AND PhD STUDENTS:
PG students (M.Tech, M.Plan, MBA, M.Sc) and PhD students must also apply through ERP and fill in their city, district, and state as per domicile certificate. If this information is not filled, they will not be considered for hostel allotment. Hostel accommodation for MBA and M.Sc first-year male students is subject to availability and is not always guaranteed.

FOR STUDENTS NOT WILLING TO TAKE POSSESSION:
If a student is allotted a hostel room but does not wish to take possession, they must immediately send an email to hosteloffice@mnit.ac.in. If not intimated, diet charges will be levied from the date of allotment.

SECTION 6: HOSTEL VACATING PROCESS (VERIFIED FROM OFFICIAL MNIT CIRCULARS 2025)
=======================================================================

SUMMER VACATION VACATING (ALL HOSTELLERS):
As per official Circular No. 1861 dated 01 May 2025 from the Office of Dean Student Welfare (Hostel Office), all UG and PG hostellers currently residing in the hostel are required to vacate their rooms before proceeding for summer vacation. This is required for maintenance of the building. Non-compliance is taken very seriously.

Students vacating for summer can deposit their baggage in the respective hostel common room. They must strictly hand over their room to the Caretaker.

STAYING DURING SUMMER VACATION:
UG students who wish to stay in the hostel during summer vacation must submit a duly filled Undertaking Form to the hostel office. A separate circular and form is issued by the hostel office each year for this purpose.

FINAL YEAR / GRADUATING STUDENTS — PERMANENT HOSTEL LEAVING:
Final year UG and PG students who are leaving the hostel permanently must submit the No-Dues and Caution Money Refund Performa to the concerned Caretaker while vacating the room. This form is issued by the Office of Dean Students Welfare (Hostel Wing).

The No-Dues Performa requires the following information: Student Name, Student ID, Mobile Number, Email ID, Parent Name and Mobile Number, Correspondence Address, Room Number, Hostel Joining Date, Hostel Leaving Date, and Bank Details for caution money refund (Account Number, IFSC Code, Bank Name and Address).

After verification by the caretaker and hostel office, the refundable caution money of INR 12,000 is returned to the student's bank account as provided in the form. The one-time admission fee of INR 3,000 is not refundable.

The remaining mess advance balance (actual unused amount from the mess advance paid) is also adjusted and refunded at the time of leaving.

SECTION 7: ROOM TYPES AND ALLOTMENT POLICY
=======================================================================

First-year UG students are allotted triple-occupancy rooms, meaning three students share one room. First-year boys are typically allotted Hostel H-01 (Parijat), H-02 (Chaitanya), H-06 (Kabir), or H-07 (Drona).

From the second year onwards, all students are allotted single-occupancy rooms. Room and hostel allotment changes every year and is done randomly based on branch and state of domicile as per the Random Hostel Allotment Policy.

Accommodation is provided on a first-come, first-served basis subject to availability of hostel rooms. Students from Jaipur city are not eligible for hostel allotment.

SECTION 8: HOSTEL FACILITIES
=======================================================================

Every hostel room at MNIT Jaipur is furnished with a bed, study table, chair, cupboard, and fan. Wi-Fi and LAN wired internet connectivity is available in all hostel rooms 24x7. Students use their LDAP ID and password to connect to the internet.

Each hostel has its own mess where food is served four times a day, which includes breakfast, lunch, snacks, and dinner. Eggs are typically provided twice a week. No non-vegetarian food other than eggs is served in the government mess. A private mess option called Mess Z is available in some hostels. The government mess food is generally rated better than the private mess by students.

Every hostel has a common room equipped with an LED HD television with DTH connection and table tennis tables. Badminton courts and open-air theatres are available in the hostel area. A fully equipped gymnasium is available for students. Laundry services are available. A night canteen is also available near the hostel zone.

The campus has 24x7 security. Girls hostels have an in-time of 10 PM. Students are not permitted to go out of the institute after 10 PM and before 6 AM as per hostel rules.

In case of theft or any emergency, students must immediately report to the concerned Warden or Chief Security Officer. For health emergencies, students should contact the institute dispensary between 8 AM and 6 PM, and the on-call Medical Officer at the campus colony for emergencies between 6 PM and 8 AM.

=======================================================================
SECTION 9: HOSTEL RULES SUMMARY
=======================================================================

Students must occupy only the room allotted to them. Room change is not permitted without written permission from the appropriate authority. Students must keep their rooms secure when leaving even for short periods. The institute is not responsible for loss due to theft. Students must not engage in any activity that disturbs other residents. Anti-ragging undertaking must be submitted by all students at the time of admission through the UGC Anti-Ragging Portal. Non-compliance with hostel rules can lead to disciplinary action.
